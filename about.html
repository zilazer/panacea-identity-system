03 / Panacea / 起源

One mark,
still evolving.

一个字标，仍在生长。

Panacea 起初是一个字标。字标中的结构逐渐被拆解、重组，形成一套建筑构件式字形，并延伸为二十六组建筑摄影字母。

Panacea began as a wordmark. Its structures were gradually taken apart and recomposed into an architectural glyph system, then extended into twenty-six photographic studies of architectural letters.

同一种语言后来进入车辆识别与其他应用。它不是一个已经完成的标志，而是一套在不同媒介中持续演变的长期视觉系统。

The same language later moved into vehicle identity and other applications. It is not a finished mark, but a long-term visual system that continues to evolve across different media.
